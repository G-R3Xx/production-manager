# Production Manager V26.09.08.02

## Quote saved-product entry

- Restored **Saved product** as the preferred/default starting point when adding a quote line.
- Added a clear **Start from** choice between Saved product and Quick / custom line.
- Saved products are separated into **Signage** and **Small format** filters with live counts.
- Plan printing, poster printing, service/install and custom component work remain in Quick / custom line so the saved-product picker stays focused.
- Selecting a department immediately selects the first product in that department and loads its saved options.
- Product SKU is shown beside the product name when available.
