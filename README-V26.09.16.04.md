# Production Manager V26.09.16.04

## Product setup flow

- Reordered product setup into the working sequence: product and stock, customer quote choices, Production Method, costing rules, then review and test.
- Small-format products now use tailored builder steps instead of the universal signage workflow.
- Small-format products must choose stock; the misleading service-only **No physical material** option is hidden.
- Customer choices are no longer described as optional and are opened by default for small-format products.
- Secondary website, advanced and removal controls are grouped under **More** to keep the main action area focused.

## Testing and price clarity

- Replaced the early saved-price panel with a final readiness checklist and production-test action.
- Renamed **Price check** to **Production test** and clearly labels its result as a Production Method/resource estimate.
- The final customer sell price is verified through a draft Quote, where stock choices, product options, small-format overhead and profit are applied.
- Small-format starter products now open with sensible test defaults: cards 90 × 55 mm, DL flyers 99 × 210 mm, and books/booklets A5.
- Re-running the testing setup safely adds missing finished-size defaults to existing small-format starters.

## Version

App and WordPress catalogue version updated to **V26.09.16.04**.
