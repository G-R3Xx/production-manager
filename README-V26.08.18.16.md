# Production Manager V26.08.18.16

## Remove legacy survey quote placeholders

- Survey to Quote no longer creates zero-price `Sign 1 / Sign 2` placeholder quote lines.
- Existing untouched survey placeholders are removed automatically when reopening that survey's quote.
- The survey stays linked at quote level for measurements, photos and notes while actual lines are created through the normal guided builder.
- The New draft panel remains collapsed when an existing survey quote is open.
