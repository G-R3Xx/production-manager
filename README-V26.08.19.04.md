# Production Manager V26.08.19.04

- Adds a sortable Received column to the Dashboard.
- Shows the age of every active job in days directly below its received date.
- Adds instant job-age filters for 0–7, 8–14, 15–30, 31–60 and 61+ days.
- Keeps age filtering and received-date sorting entirely browser-side without page reloads.
- Tracks the received timestamp from the earliest linked enquiry, survey, quote, artwork or production record.
