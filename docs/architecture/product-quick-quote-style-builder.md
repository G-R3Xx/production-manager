# Product quick-quote style builder — V26.07.29.02

The normal Product Build tab now mirrors the successful Quick Quote interaction model.

- All material, size, print, finishing and fulfilment choices load once.
- Step navigation and option selection are client-side and do not refresh the route.
- The product is saved once from the final review step.
- The same saved defaults become internal quote fields and WordPress fields.
- Eyelet placement presets use the same follow-up quantity model as Quick Quote.
- Advanced recipe sequencing and raw rules remain available without being required for normal product setup.
