# Internal product workflow

Version: V26.07.28.12

## Priority

Production Manager is an internal quoting and production system first. Website publishing is an optional output of a completed product, not a prerequisite for creating one.

## Everyday product setup

1. Choose the main material.
2. Enter the normal finished width, height and quantity.
3. Choose no print, direct print or roll print.
4. Select normal finishing actions.
5. Choose pickup, delivery or install.
6. Save and make the product available for quoting.

The save action maintains the underlying production recipe and the standard quote fields for size, quantity, print method and fulfilment.

## Advanced setup

Machines, labour rules, reusable Production Steps, Manufacturing Methods and complex configurator dependencies stay available under Settings and Advanced product tools. They do not load during normal product setup.
