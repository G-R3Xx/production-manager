# Signage laminate whole-metre nesting batch

## Change

Signage laminate usage now calculates from the whole quote-line quantity instead of only the single-unit size.

The laminate roll length is nested across the roll width and then rounded up to the next full metre.

Examples on a 1220mm laminate roll:

- 1 × 600 × 900mm = 1lm charged
- 2 × 600 × 900mm = 1lm charged because two 600mm panels fit across the roll width
- 3 × 600 × 900mm = 2lm charged
- 4 × 600 × 900mm = 2lm charged
- 2400mm length = 3lm charged
- 1200mm length = 2lm charged

## Notes

The quote review still shows a unit sell price, so the total rounded laminate metres are divided back across the quote quantity for the unit price calculation. The breakdown note shows the total charged laminate metres for clarity.
