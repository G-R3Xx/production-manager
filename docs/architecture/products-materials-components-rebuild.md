# Products, Materials, Components and Options Rebuild

This rebuild keeps the workflow centred on Clients, Suppliers, Materials, Products, Quotes and Integrations.

## Locked direction

- Suppliers sell materials.
- Materials are purchased stock.
- Products are sellable quote items.
- Product setup happens in Products only.
- Components and options sit behind products.
- Stock allocation should consume materials, not finished products.
- Product tax code defaults to GST and is hidden from product creation.

## Product setup

The Products page now contains:

- Product details editor.
- Quick setup presets for rigid signage, roll print, cards, books and carbon books.
- Component rules for fixed, per-unit, per-sqm, per-linear-metre, per-sheet, yield-based and option-triggered usage.
- Option rules for sizes, sides, laminate, cello, binding, duplicate/triplicate copy sets, copy colours, cover colour, tape colour, quantity and page count.

## Materials setup

The Materials page now treats materials as purchased stock and supports sheet media, roll media, roll laminate, paper stock, card stock, cello, binding, finishing consumables, fixings, items and other stock.
