# Production Manager V26.08.19.19

- Rebuilds Calendar as an interactive operational staff planner, with Week, Month and 30-day Agenda views.
- Makes the Week view staff-first: staff are rows, dates are columns, and multi-staff work appears for every assigned person.
- Synthesises each active job's current process so work missing a saved assignment or due date is still visible.
- Adds an Unscheduled work queue for every visible active process or extra task without a date.
- Supports drag-to-reschedule from the calendar or directly from the Unscheduled queue.
- Adds a no-reload schedule drawer for setting due dates and assigning one or multiple staff.
- Preserves Production and Dispatch inheritance when their process assignments are changed from Calendar.
- Adds instant search plus staff, process, job-type, overdue and completed-work filters without route reloads.
- Colour-codes Enquiry, Survey, Quote, Artwork, Production, Dispatch, Invoicing and extra tasks consistently.
- Links every calendar item back to its complete Job workspace.
